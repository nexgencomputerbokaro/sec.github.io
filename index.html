About NexGen Computer

Welcome to Nexgen Computer Training Institute, Bokaro’s premier destination for comprehensive computer and IT education since 2016. Our mission is to empower learners from all backgrounds with industry-relevant skills to excel in today’s digital world.

At Nexgen, we offer a wide array of professional courses including Basic Computer Education, Diploma Programs (ADCA, DCA), Advanced Excel & Data Analysis, Programming Languages like Python and C++, Web Development, SQL, and Graphics Designing. Designed with a practical, hands-on approach, our courses cater to students, working professionals, and anyone looking to upgrade their IT skills for better career opportunities.
